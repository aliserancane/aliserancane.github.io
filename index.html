About

Alise Rancāne (1995) is a Latvian contemporary music composer working across acoustic and electroacoustic music.

She writes for chamber ensembles, creates electronic music scores, and experiments with sound and space through interdisciplinary projects and sound installations. Her practice also includes collaborations with visual artists and music for theatre.

In 2021, she graduated from the Jāzeps Vītols Latvian Academy of Music with a master’s degree in composition, where she studied with Jānis Petraškevičs. As part of the Erasmus programme, she studied composition with Per Mårtensson and electroacoustic music with Mattias Petersson at the Royal College of Music in Stockholm.

She also performs electronic music and is interested in real-time music programming and evolving approaches to electronic music performance.
